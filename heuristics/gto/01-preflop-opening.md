# Preflop opening ranges

Roughly, at 100bb six-max, a competent regular opens:

| position | share of hands |
|---|---|
| UTG | 15–18% |
| HJ | 19–22% |
| CO | 25–28% |
| BTN | 40–48% |
| SB | 35–45% (raise-or-fold) |

The shape matters more than the number. Early position is **linear** — the
best hands by raw strength, pairs and big broadways, few suited connectors.
The button is **wide and connected** — every suited ace, most suited gappers,
weak offsuit broadways.

Signals that shift it:

- **A limp instead of a raise** is rarely a strong hand in a regular's range,
  but is common in this pool with small and medium pairs and suited aces.
- **An open from a short stack** (under 40bb) is tighter and more linear.
- **Opening after several limpers** widens toward hands that flop well
  multiway: pairs, suited aces, suited connectors — not offsuit broadways.
