# Postflop: who the board favours

A range does not hit a board evenly. Before reading anyone's actions, ask which
range the texture favours.

- **Ace-high and king-high dry boards** favour the preflop raiser. They hold
  more aces and kings, and the caller folded most weak aces preflop.
- **Middling connected boards** (9-8-6, J-T-7) favour the caller. Two pair and
  straights live in the hands that call rather than raise.
- **Paired boards** favour whoever has more of that rank, which is usually the
  raiser on high pairs and the caller on low ones.
- **Monotone boards** compress everything: the range with more of that suit has
  a large advantage, and nobody folds a made flush.

From this, continuation betting:

- A **small bet on a dry board hero could have hit** is a wide range, close to
  everything, and says little.
- A **large bet on a connected board** is polarised: strong made hands and
  draws, with the middle checking.
- A **check from the preflop raiser** is not weakness by default; strong hands
  check on boards that favour the caller.

Later streets narrow ranges fast. A player who bets three streets on a board
that never completed an obvious draw has very few bluffs unless they are
deliberately balanced — and most of this pool is not.
