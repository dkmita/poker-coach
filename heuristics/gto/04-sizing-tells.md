# What bet sizing implies

Sizing is the most reliable structural signal available without a read, because
it is chosen before the opponent reacts.

- **Under a third of the pot** — wide and thin. A range bet, or a hand that
  wants a cheap look. Very few nutted hands, but they exist.
- **Half to two-thirds** — the default. Says the least of any size.
- **Pot or more** — polarised. Strong hands and give-up bluffs, little between.
  On the river, closer to pure: either the nuts or nothing.
- **All-in over-bet on the river** — in this pool, weighted heavily to value.
  Balanced over-bet bluffing is rare below mid-stakes.

Two cautions:

- **Sizing tells are strategy-dependent.** A player who uses one size for
  everything gives away nothing by size, and their range at that size is their
  whole continuing range.
- **A small bet is not a small range.** It usually means a *wider* one, which
  makes it weaker on average but not weak in the tail.
